              What the bot knows

              Start the bot to hear what it knows.
              Long-run memory.

              The long-run memory keeps every price bar the bot sees and, once a day outside trading hours, replays all of them through every strategy. It feeds these reports, the probability calculator and the decision lab (Ask tab). Under the evidence rule it also backs up the recent knowledge base (What works) when the bot decides a trade; every risk rule applies either way.

              How well it predicts

              Loading…

              Every prediction is saved with its outcome. When it said 60%, about 60% should have won: bars close to the line mean its numbers can be trusted. Skill above 0 means it beats always guessing the average win rate. Training replays are scored day by day using only the days before, like live.
