          Settings

          Changes apply immediately, are saved, and are listed in Activity. Bounds keep every value safe for your plan. config.yaml is not rewritten here — “Undo all changes” goes back to it. Your account, market, strategy and the risk the bot starts with are on the ; Topstep's own rules never change.

          Phone access (Telegram)

          Opens this dashboard inside Telegram on your phone when you're away from the PC. Only your own Telegram account can open it: Telegram signs you in and everyone else is refused. The bot keeps running and trading on this PC from your own internet connection; a secure Cloudflare link carries only the dashboard to your phone, with no router changes. On the phone you can do everything you can here except switch to LIVE and turn phone access on. In Telegram, /dashboard opens it and /dashboard off turns it off.
