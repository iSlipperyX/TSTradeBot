What works when

Average result per signal in R (1R = the risk of that trade) and the number of signals seen, by time of day (Chicago) and volatility regime. ✔ the Adaptive strategy trades it now, ✘ switched off because it has been losing, ? not enough evidence yet. The bot adds every signal it sees — traded or not — while it runs, and retrains on recent history each day. Older evidence fades out.

What the bot has learned

Average result per signal in R. After costs takes out fees and, for ideas that weren't traded, the slippage your backtests assume; real fills already include their slippage. Best / worst point: how far a signal went for and against it on average before it ended. Losers up 1R first: losing signals that were 1R in profit on the way, a sign a breakeven stop or a target might help. Observations from before these were measured count before costs.

    Hints, not proven: the bot compares many strategies on many measurements, so some differences are luck. Nothing here changes how the bot trades; a condition earns a place in its decisions only after it holds up on data it never saw. Faded numbers have fewer than 15 observations.

    Your manual trades

    Trades you placed from the Trade tab, by time of day and regime. They never switch a strategy on or off; the trade ticket shows your record next to the strategies' so you can see where your own trades work.

    Settings

    Changes apply immediately, are saved, and are listed in Activity. Bounds keep every value safe for your plan. config.yaml is never rewritten — “Undo all changes” goes back to it. Topstep's own rules, the account and the symbol can only be changed on your PC.
